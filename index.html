Aaj Kya? ("What today?") is a set of small, fast tools for the maths that comes up every day in India — working out a discount at a sale, splitting a restaurant bill, checking GST on an invoice, or counting the days until a wedding. No app to install, no account to create. Just open a tool, type your numbers, and get your answer.
